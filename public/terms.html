Terms of Use

These terms apply when you use The House – Koh Tao digital guest guide, secure registration and AI Concierge.

Effective: 26 August 2026

Using the service

The service is provided for current and prospective guests of The House. Please provide accurate information, use protected links only for the booked room and do not attempt to access another guest’s information or interfere with the service.

Guest registration and protected access

Non-Thai overnight guests must provide the information needed for required accommodation registration. Thai nationals are exempt from that passport-registration flow. Protected room information is available only after the applicable stay and registration checks are complete. Do not share private room links, confirmation details or protected access information.

AI Concierge

The AI Concierge provides practical answers from approved House information and can route requests to the relevant team. It may occasionally misunderstand a question or provide incomplete information. Confirm anything safety-critical, time-sensitive or personally important with a human. The concierge cannot independently authorize protected access.

Bookings and third-party services

The House may help coordinate activities, transport or other services. Availability can change, and the service provider’s own safety requirements, eligibility rules, cancellation terms and charges may apply. Ask the House Concierge when you need current details or assistance.

Emergencies

The guest guide and AI Concierge are not emergency services. For an immediate local emergency, contact Koh Tao Rescue first or Thailand’s medical emergency service on 1669. The system does not automatically call emergency services for you.

House rules, fees and availability

Guests remain responsible for the booking terms, House rules and clearly communicated charges that apply to their stay. The service may be updated, interrupted or temporarily unavailable. If a digital feature is unavailable, contact the House team for assistance.
