Why we recommend Roctopus

Roctopus is known for experienced instructors, small training groups, strong safety standards and a friendly international atmosphere. Reviews frequently praise the personalised instruction, professional organisation and supportive approach for both first-time and certified divers.

They also place meaningful emphasis on marine conservation, responsible diving and helping guests improve their skills while enjoying Koh Tao’s dive sites.

Responsible diving and environmental awareness.
