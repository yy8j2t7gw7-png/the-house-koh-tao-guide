Diving on Koh Tao

The House's preferred recommendation is Roctopus Dive, a Dive RAID International centre known for a published maximum of four divers per instructor or dive leader on its core recreational training and fun-diving programme.

Important: Roctopus is RAID, not PADI. Koh Tao also has excellent SSI and PADI centres; the right choice depends on training agency preference, location, instructor fit, group size and course goals.
