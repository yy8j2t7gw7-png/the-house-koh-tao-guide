Why we recommend Roctopus

Roctopus is known for experienced instructors, small training groups, strong safety standards and a friendly international atmosphere. Reviews regularly highlight the personalised instruction, clear organisation and supportive approach for both first-time and certified divers.

They also place real emphasis on responsible diving, marine conservation and helping guests develop good underwater skills.
