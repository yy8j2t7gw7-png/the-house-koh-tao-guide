Privacy request

Data-deletion instructions

You may ask The House – Koh Tao to delete eligible personal information connected with your stay or use of the guest service.

Effective: 26 August 2026

Send your request

Email thehousekohtao@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.

Email a deletion request

Information that helps us find the correct record

  • Your full name as used for the booking.
  • Your approximate check-in and checkout dates.
  • Your room number, if known.
  • The booking platform or direct-booking method.
  • A safe email address where we can respond.

Do not email a password, booking confirmation code, key code, passport image or payment-card information.

Verification and processing

We may ask for limited additional information through a safe channel to confirm that the request relates to you. After verification, we will delete eligible information and confirm the outcome. Some reservation, accommodation-registration, security or dispute records may need to be retained when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect guests and the property.

Automatic deletion already in place

  • Passport files are automatically deleted no later than 14 days after upload and may be removed sooner after processing.
  • Concierge interactions, feedback, operational alerts and optional maintenance images are normally retained for 30 days or less.
  • Short conversation context kept in the browser ends with the browser session.

See the Privacy Policy for more detail.
