# Superseded: Secure After-Hours Spare-Key Access

The time-based policy in earlier releases is obsolete. Since v5.11.22, verified guests may use the protected lost-key self-service flow 24 hours a day.

Do not use office, housekeeping, reception or other service hours as an authorization gate. Those schedules remain unchanged for their own workflows.

The authoritative specification is `SECURE_24_HOUR_LOST_KEY_ACCESS.md`.
