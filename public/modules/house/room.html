Required guest registration

This TM30 Immigration accommodation registration applies only to non-Thai guests. Thai nationals do not need to upload a passport. If any non-Thai guest is staying overnight, securely upload a passport for each one.

Choose the Thai-national exemption only when every overnight guest on this reservation is Thai. Passport images use a private, room-bound, single-use upload form and are automatically deleted 14 days after upload, or sooner after processing. Never send passport information in the concierge chat or WhatsApp.

Protected after-hours access

Lost key or locked out?

After hours—from 7:30 PM until 10:30 AM Bangkok time—a verified guest can obtain the spare-key code for this room. The key box is directly next to the room door.

A lost-key replacement fee of 500 THB will be added. The request is recorded and our urgent team is notified immediately. The key-box code is never included in that notification.
