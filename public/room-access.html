Welcome to The House

Complete secure guest verification to open your private room guide. Room location, arrival photos, Wi-Fi and stay instructions remain protected until this is complete.

Secure guest access

Verify your stay

Enter the Airbnb confirmation code shown in your trip details, or the private House stay code provided for a direct booking. This confirms that this permanent link belongs to your booked room.

Usually begins with HM. The code is checked securely and is never stored in readable form.

Who is staying overnight?

Choose the option that applies to everyone staying in the room. If the group includes both Thai and foreign guests, choose Foreign guest(s).

Thai nationals only

This TM30 Immigration accommodation registration applies only to non-Thai guests. Thai nationals do not need to upload a passport. No passport information is required when every overnight guest is a Thai national.

Foreign guest(s)

Passport information is required for every non-Thai person staying overnight, not only the guest who made the booking.

The information is required for Thailand's TM30 accommodation registration. Passport images are stored privately, never sent through the AI Concierge or WhatsApp, and deleted automatically 14 days after upload—or sooner after processing.

Choose how to provide passport information

Every non-Thai overnight guest must be submitted. This includes partners, family members, friends and children staying in the room, not only the Airbnb booking guest.

Upload passports securely

Use one private, room-bound and single-use form for each non-Thai overnight guest. Passport images are automatically deleted 14 days after upload—or sooner after processing.

Provide passports in person

Bring the original passports of every non-Thai adult and child staying overnight to The House. No passport image upload is needed when you choose this option.

The required details are used only for Thailand's TM30 accommodation registration. The private room guide opens after all secure uploads are received, or after our team confirms the in-person passport check and registration are complete.

Need urgent help?

Emergency information remains available without guest verification. The public concierge can also guide you through verification and remind you to submit passport information if it has not been completed.
