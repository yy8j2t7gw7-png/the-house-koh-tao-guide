Beaches & Bays

From easy sunset beaches to remote snorkelling bays, Koh Tao offers a different coastline for every kind of day.

Before you go: Wind, waves, tides, road access and entrance fees can change. If one side of the island is rough, another side may be calmer. Carry cash, avoid touching coral or wildlife, and never ride a scooter beyond your experience level.
